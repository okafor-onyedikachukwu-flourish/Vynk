Vynk

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what Vynk collects, why, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, write to vynkcontact01@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the name you choose, an optional profile photo and an optional “About” line.
  • What you send: messages, photos, files, GIFs and emoji reactions you share in chats, plus when they were sent and read.
  • Activity: when you were last active, which powers “Online” and “Last seen”.
  • Device token: a notification token so we can deliver message alerts to your phone.
  • Safety records: the people you block and any reports you make.

We don’t ask for your phone number, your contacts or your location.

How we use it

Only to run Vynk: to create your account, deliver messages and notifications, let people find you when they already know your exact email address, show read receipts and typing, and investigate reports of abuse. We don’t sell your information and we don’t show ads.

Who can see what

  • Your name, photo, About line and email are visible to people you share a chat with, and to anyone who searches for your exact email address.
  • Messages, photos and files are visible only to the members of the chat they were sent in.
  • People who block you can no longer find you, and you can block anyone in the same way.

Security

Messages and files travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and are stored on secured servers, with database rules that limit each chat to its members. Vynk does not currently use end-to-end encryption, which means Vynk’s systems can technically access stored messages. We access them only when needed to operate the service, investigate a report, or meet a legal obligation.

Service providers

We use Supabase to store accounts, messages and files, and Expo’s push service (which uses Apple and Google notification services) to deliver notifications. GIF search is provided by KLIPY: when you search for GIFs, your search words and an anonymous ID (never your name or email) are sent to KLIPY, and GIFs in chats load from KLIPY’s servers when viewed. These providers process data on our behalf to provide these services.

Stored on your phone

So Vynk works without a connection, the app keeps a copy of your chat list and the most recent messages in each chat on your phone, along with any messages waiting to be sent. Signing out erases all of it from the phone.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. When you delete a message for everyone, its content and any attached file are removed. When you delete your account, your profile, photo, chat memberships, blocks and notification tokens are removed. Messages you already sent remain visible to the people who received them, labelled “Deleted account”.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, photo and About line in Settings.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone’s settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings, or ask us to do it by email.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by email.

Children

Vynk is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required by law where you live. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll update the date above and let you know in the app.
