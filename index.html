Message

Your phone number stays yours.

Vynk never asks for it. People reach you through the email you choose to share, and nobody can browse the list of people on Vynk.

Everything you expect from a messaging app.

One-to-one chats

Type the exact email someone signed up with and start talking. If they aren’t on Vynk yet, send them an invite.

Groups of up to 256

Admins add people by email, rename the group and remove members. Anyone can leave whenever they like.

Photos, files and GIFs

Share pictures from your camera or library, documents up to 25 MB, and GIFs from KLIPY’s library.

Reactions, receipts and typing

React to any message with an emoji. Two brass ticks mean your message was read, and you’ll see when someone is typing.

Notifications

New messages arrive as notifications, even when Vynk is closed. Tap one to jump straight into the chat.

Block and report

Block anyone in one tap and they can no longer message you or find you by email. Reports go to the Vynk team.

Start chatting in three steps.

  1. Create your account

    Download Vynk and sign up with your name, email address and a password.

  2. Confirm your email

    Enter the code we email you. Only confirmed accounts can be found by others.

  3. Find your people

    Enter a friend’s email to start a chat, or add several people to a group.

What happens to your messages.

Only the people in a chat can read it. Messages travel over encrypted connections and are stored on secured servers. Photos and files are private links that work only for chat members.

You stay in control. Delete a message for everyone, block people, or delete your account from Settings at any time.

Plainly stated: Vynk does not use end-to-end encryption yet. We’ll say so here when that changes.

Questions

Do I need a phone number?

No. You sign up with an email address and a password. Vynk never asks for your phone number.

Can strangers find me?

Only someone who already knows your exact email address can find you. There’s no public directory and no way to search by name.

Which phones does Vynk work on?

Vynk is available for iPhone and Android phones.

What if the person I want to message isn’t on Vynk?

Vynk tells you no account uses that email and lets you send them an invite from your phone.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. Your profile is removed and you leave every chat. Messages you already sent stay with the people who received them.
